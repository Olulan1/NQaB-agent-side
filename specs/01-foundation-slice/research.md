# Research

## Intent
Validate the core loop of the game as a playable corridor action slice in Godot.

## Focus
- Player movement, jump, crouch, and firing
- One test corridor
- One enemy type
- One elevator transition
- Basic health and damage

## Open questions
- Is the corridor width comfortable for jump/crouch combat?
- Does the blaster feel readable before adding more weapons?
- Is one enemy enough to prove the pacing?
