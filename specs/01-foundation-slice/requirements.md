# Requirements

## Must have
- A Godot project that runs the first slice from a main scene.
- A player that can move, jump, crouch, and shoot.
- One short corridor room for testing combat and traversal.
- One enemy with a simple attack pattern.
- An elevator or exit transition into the next room.

## Should have
- A visible health readout.
- One pickup or healing item if needed to keep the slice playable.

## Out of scope
- Full 20-floor content.
- Multiple weapon types.
- Complex drop tables.
- Backtracking systems.
